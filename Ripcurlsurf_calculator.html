    Guide

    The display is a text field. Type directly, tap keys, or mix both. Keys marked in gold above a key are its 2nd function: press 2nd, then the key.

    Syntax

    2π, 3(4+1), 2sin 30
    Implicit multiplication. It binds tighter than ÷, so 1/2π equals 1/(2π).
    −2² = −4
    Powers bind before a leading minus. Powers chain right to left: 2^3^2 = 2^9.
    6.022E23
    Scientific notation from the EXP key, or type e/E.
    sin(30°)
    The ° mark reads the value as degrees in any angle mode.
    r = 4
    Stores a variable. Ans holds the last result; M holds memory.
    log(x, b), root(x, n)
    Logarithm in base b, and the n-th root.
    nCr(n, r), nPr(n, r)
    Combinations and permutations. x! works for non-integers through the gamma function.
    a mod b
    Remainder with the sign of b; also mod(a, b).
